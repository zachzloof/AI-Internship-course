**Q: What is the blockchain?**

Expected: The blockchain is a public, distributed ledger that records every Bitcoin transaction; nodes each keep a copy, and transactions are grouped into cryptographically linked blocks.

This design makes it extremely difficult to alter past transactions, as doing so would require redoing the work for every subsequent block on a majority of the network's computing power simultaneously.
